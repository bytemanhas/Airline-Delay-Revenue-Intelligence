# ✈️ Airline Delay & Revenue Intelligence Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

The Airline Delay & Revenue Intelligence Platform is a Python-based data analytics project designed to analyze airline operations, identify delay patterns, evaluate route profitability, and measure the financial impact of operational inefficiencies.

The project integrates multiple aviation datasets and applies data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), KPI development, and business intelligence techniques to generate actionable insights for airline operations and management.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze flight delay patterns across airlines and routes.
* Identify the most common causes of delays.
* Evaluate airline operational performance.
* Measure route profitability.
* Estimate revenue loss caused by delays.
* Develop data-driven recommendations for improving airline efficiency.

* Revenue by Route

---

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Delay Type Analysis

Analyzed the frequency of different delay categories such as weather, technical issues, crew shortages, and air traffic disruptions.

### 2. Airline Performance Analysis

Compared average delay times across airlines to identify operational strengths and weaknesses.

### 3. Revenue Analysis

Evaluated airline and route-level revenue performance.

### 4. Monthly Delay Trend Analysis

Studied delay trends over time to identify seasonal patterns.

### 5. Route Profitability Analysis

Determined which routes generated the highest revenue.

### 6. Delay vs Revenue Analysis

Examined the relationship between operational delays and revenue generation.

### 7. Route Risk Matrix

Identified high-risk and high-opportunity routes based on delay and revenue metrics.

### 8. Airline Efficiency Score

Developed a custom efficiency metric to compare airline performance.

### 9. Revenue Loss Analysis

Estimated the financial impact of delays on airline operations.
